# Art's taste and past rejections

Read before any visual work. One line per lesson; add new ones with `/handoff`.

## Process
- Read the page text closely before inventing a concept. The scene follows it phrase by phrase.
  (hear-state v1, states as floating cards: «потерял смысл текста».)
- On practice pages never show the reader where or what they feel. Give a neutral prompt
  (a scan, a question) and leave the answer to them. Fewer waves, calmer. (hear-state v2.)
- Each page needs its own image, manner and camera. Pages 04–06 of block 1 and 01–03 of block 2
  grew from one crate (dark room with pillars and grid floor, seated person in profile, card
  bottom-right in Disket Mono, thin-lens focus pulls); Art: «все анимации стали однотипные».
  Offer 2–3 distinct directions, recommend one. (persona-belief v1 rejected, «Оттиск» chosen.)
- Approve Тишина first, then the other 6 themes. Never do all themes at once.
- Live HTML prototype instead of video (videos took ~50 min to render and he had to wait).
- Approved changes often ship together. When he says «пушим всё вместе», wait for every part.

## Look
- Light themes: the scene merges completely with the page. A paler rectangle reads as a white
  background («почему все еще белый фон есть?»). Light is never white: «белый цвет там вообще
  не в тему»; ink it in the lamp color, like the amber/umber effects of his light paintings.
- Дюна: no eye-searing saturated orange; soft sepia print.
- Light themes printed from a light painting read «ярковаты слишком» in full colour; a muted
  two-ink print per theme (deep + mid ink, paper = page) was approved. (Step 5 hero.)
- No painted effect that duplicates an animation: the bird's static sound arcs had to go once
  the bird sang in motion («висит постоянно»). (Step 5 hero.)
- A dark painting with no light pair: Rust prints it on light themes as a soft engraving (black
  becomes the page, light becomes ink); he chose this over painting a light one. (Step 5 page 01.)
- Tablet/PC: illustrations as wide as the plate below, never a centered shrunk hero.
- He does not like the light themes and thinks of removing them from the site («мне они не
  нравятся»); page 07 shipped with them unadapted. Ask before spending work on light themes.
- Words placed literally where he says: the tuning-questions conclusion sits at lip height.
- Hairlines must survive 125% display scaling (draw outlines outside the clip path).

## World in glasses (block 1 page 01)
- Gum stays GOLD (pink clashes). No smile at the end. No grey/beige desaturation in the cracks.
- Lens edges come from his hand-drawn mask (`graphics/world-in-glasses/assets/lens-mask.svg`). Never auto-trace lenses.

## Колокол (block 2 page 04)
- Light themes must read as clearly as the 48 s flood frame: «слишком всё сливается» when only faint lamp lines sat on the page. Fix: a quiet light from the mouth and an ink S-curve (lit forms deepen to ink).

## Пламя (block 2 page 07)
- A separate wax doll melting beside the candle made no sense to him («странно… стоит отдельно и тает»). The candle itself is the person: a wax figure with the wick in its crown, melting into a plain candle.

## Глаз (block 2 page 08)
- First take (one quiet eye) was «скучно»; he picked a mix: dreams on the lid, the world turning into a grid, a person in the pupil.
- When the camera dives into something, it must go all the way in: «глаза больше не было». Nothing of the outer object left in frame.
- Figures of light: a clean, well-proportioned silhouette, no patterns or fibre weaves («непонятные узоры», «топорная»).
- Torn/ragged transitions look crude («разрывы выглядят топорно»); soft melts (ripple, blur, fade) passed.
- On light themes a light figure must not turn into a solid ink shadow: inked edge, lamp-tinted body.

## Step 2 last page «Лабиринт»
- The old closing scene was too plain and its person «криво»: he wants «дороже, глубже и интереснее» and a properly drawn human figure.
- He chose a labyrinth told by the camera: first person inside, then a pull back over the plan; the figure of light at the end breaks the labyrinth.
- A light figure in a one-hue theme (Матрица, Искра) clips into a blot: dim the body, ink the drawing paler.

## Step 5 «Внешнее кольцо»
- Painted panoramas want depth and a living head: parallax from a depth map, a small sway, leaning toward where something happens. A static raster element (the bird painted in the sky) «выглядит тупо»: keep the camera away from it. (Page 01.)
- «Якорь» first take lacked detail, depth and breathing; a crooked outline read badly. Wanted: a background (Matrix-like rain is fine), breathing, an even outline. (Page 02.)
- A loop must not open with a long empty beat: «9 секунд без сюжета это слишком много». The lone point of page 04 now lasts about 4 s before it opens. (Page 04.)
- A dome is built from the top down: the crown first, then belts below it. Every step must come in softly, the first one too («остальные появляются мягко, а первый резко»). More Matrix detail behind: code rain, projections. (Page 05.)
- He liked the floor projection rings; at the frame beat (42 s) they should spin «красиво… в разные стороны, словно произошло полное включение». (Page 05.)
- A stilling story needs a dramatic start: a soft ripple was too quiet and drew the eye in the wrong way («ряб очень сильно перенимает внимание»); he asked for big waves, wind and rain, «что-то типа шторма почти». Each calming step must read on its own; the rain fades in two steps (weaker at the first «Не», gone at the second). (Page 06.)
- A flat walk along a painted strip with a ball floating beside the camera, and stripes of light on the glass, read as «нелепо». He liked the sphere in the middle of the ring of places («сфера и вокруг картины по кругу»), so the whole loop now lives in that ring. (Page 07.)
- Places that just light up on the ring were not enough: «сфера как бы проецировала эти изображения». The ball became a projector (lamp, beams in the air, a blank ring screen). A lamp drowns against a bright painting in the dark recolor: keep the thrown place to the side so the lamp stands against the dark. (Page 07.)
- A sequence of places reads better one at a time: each switches on as the next one switches off, and all of them come on together only at the closing run round the ring. (Page 07.)

## Step 6 «Внутреннее кольцо»
- A twinkle that chops the figure's lines into dashes reads as «белые шумы по человеку»: sparkle only point stars of the background, never the figure or the cube. (Hero.)
- A ripple must come from where its cause is: «волна должна исходить не от рандомной точки пола а от ног». (Hero.)
- An «inner» scene must show the inside being scanned: «нехватает сканирования внутреннего»; a plane of light now passes through the figure head to feet and lights its inner lines. (Hero.)
- Page 01 «Срез» rejected 2026-11-07: «странно всё выглядит и тексту мало соответствует». A body in
  height lines, cut by a plane, with abstract disturbances (funnel, crosshair, trefoil) read as odd
  and not as the text (orbit of perception, going inward, inner ring against the outer one).
- A seated person sits normally on a chair with a straight back, never cross-legged or in lotus
  («он должен нормально сидеть на стуле с прямой спиной»). Applies to every Step 6 figure.
- Page 01 «Внутрь» (Б «Эхо», rounds 2–5): presence is spherical and the outer ring keeps
  turning («Сферичное!»); no filled spheres; exactly two rings, outer and inner, nothing
  extra; the inner ring scans the whole person, calmly, with no jerky jumps between body
  parts; on «Без проваливания» the body vanishes and the head stays, never a second person;
  «образы себя» are different images of himself, not a mirrored copy; the «Без…» beats get
  the most time. Passed and shipped 2026-11-07.

## Step 7 «Динамическая сфера»
- The finale must be all 3D and «прям оочень качественное исполнение»; he judges the scene itself, not its color («я не про цвет говорил а про сцену саму»).
- Too much at once at the start was rejected: slower rings, a calm opening, then one thing scanned, then one wire deflected, «постепенно всё больше и больше». (Hero and page 01.)
- A first-person view must look round: he missed head turns left and right; about 45° each way, eased, passed. (Page 01.)
- A loop of simple actions (sit, rise, walk, sit) lacked plot: «не хватает сюжета». Work at a laptop, a talk with a second person and work again passed, and the rings and glass glowed softer so the action led. (Page 02.)
- A scene must plainly belong to its page text: «как оно к тексту относится вообще?». A top view where all the rings cover the figure confused him. A low side camera with only the ring the text names (ring 1) lit passed. (Page 03.)
- For page 04 he chose a scene with a plot (Б: get pulled in, the scanner brings you back) over the recommended calm one (А: beads of facts). Drama over a quiet catalogue. (Page 04.)
- Something inside must visibly act on the body: a sprout alone was not enough, «оно реально как будто тянуло человека куда-то». A thought lifts him and melts him from the feet up. (Page 04.)
- Give each event time before the scanner closes it («почти ничего не успевает произойти»): 3–4 s from flare to touch passed. Being pulled in must not stop the walk («на мысли человек не должен останавливаться»). (Page 04.)
- In messages, name the rings by number (первое, второе, третье кольцо). Working names confuse him: «почему ты начал вместо кольца делать корону?». (Page 05.)
- Page 05 «Темп дня» passed in Матрица on the first showing (direction А: a day of actions, ring 3 spinning with the body's tempo). (Page 05.)
- After page 05 the sphere world tired him («эта уже надоела»): pages 06–10 get a new scene, «что-то новое, интереснее», with a layered camera (first person → over the shoulder → pulled wide, «расхлопнуто») and «больше красивых деталей которые добавят глубины и стиля». (2026-11-08.)
- «Ночной город», first showing: a coat flap that stuck out read as «отросток»; he wants a proper raincoat that hangs and sways with the walk. (Page 06.)
- Cars as bare light streaks were not enough («почему только огоньки»): cars need bodies, with headlights and tail lights on them. (Page 06.)
- One walker in an empty street is too little: he asked for two more, different people (here: one under an umbrella, one looking at a phone). (Page 06.)
- He missed the sphere and its three rings in the new scene («кольца ты куда дел со сферой?»): they stay, but «еле заметные», so the city leads. (Page 06.)
- The Матрица look needs code rain on the buildings and elsewhere («нехватает матричного дождя вдоль зданий»). (Page 06.)
- His idea: whatever enters the sphere shows its code, its «матричная суть». (Page 06.)
- Round 2 with all six notes done passed at once («Супер!»), and he wanted the same scene on the home card when Step 7 is open: a liked page scene can become the step's home card. (Page 06, 2026-11-08.)
- For page 07 he picked А «Автопилот на перекрёстке» (drama again: drop into the phone, the car, the return chain) over the calmer three-checks and shop-window reflection ideas. (Page 07, 2026-11-08.)
- First showing of page 07: not just the drop and the return chain; the car must almost hit him, then time runs back and the right run plays (phone used with the rings kept, put away before the road, the five restored, wait for green, walk). A lesson shown as wrong take, rewind, right take. (Page 07, 2026-11-08.)

## Accounts and security (Intensive chat, 2026-11-07)
- Walkers get crypto-wallet simplicity: words or key plus a PIN, no forced 2FA («лишняя нагрузка»); extra layers are opt-in. Guides and admins must use 2FA.
- Private chats need strong protection: email-only access is «слабо»; nothing may reach other hands.
- Walkers may be in Russia; check that every service the app uses works there.
- The first chat UI read as «сыроватый прототип»: he wants messenger quality (Telegram as the model) built from the diary's own components (glass, framed contours, bottom sheet, Disket/Tektur roles), not new ad-hoc parts.
- The first admin page was «идея нравится исполнение кривоватое»: empty columns, small rows and a cramped layout. Admin tools need real UI/UX care: big rows, no wasted columns, master-detail (list + side card), clear filters and bulk actions.
- The admin page speaks formally: «в админке более уважительный тон, не на "ты"». Walker screens keep «ты».
- Chat details he asked for: no cut top corner on follow-up bubbles (both sides), no member icons over the chat, no «переписка зашифрована» footer; show «изменено» on edited messages.
- Admin page: no avatars at all (letters in frames read as noise in a table); plain square check boxes, not the diary's cut icon frame; refresh line left of the search.
- Two framed panes side by side: the corners that face each other stay square («вернуть углы»), the outer cuts stay.
- The same in a run of chat bubbles: no cuts where two bubbles of one writer meet, only the outer corners of the run.
- A lock icon for «close/sign out» reads wrong («странное решение»): use a sign-out door. Check boxes stay small (14 px; 20 px was «огромные»).
- Icons inside fields keep the same inset as the text («че ты её прижал?»); a control above a pane takes that pane's width.
- People are named by first name and surname («имена сделать в 2 слова» → «Имя и фамилия»).

## App icon (2026-11-07)
