# Art's taste and past rejections

Read before any visual work. One line per lesson; add new ones with `/handoff`.

## Process
- Read the page text closely before inventing a concept. The scene follows it phrase by phrase.
  (hear-state v1, states as floating cards: «потерял смысл текста».)
- On practice pages never show the reader where or what they feel. Give a neutral prompt
  (a scan, a question) and leave the answer to them. Fewer waves, calmer. (hear-state v2.)
- Each page needs its own image, manner and camera. Pages 04–06 of block 1 and 01–03 of block 2
  grew from one crate (dark room with pillars and grid floor, seated person in profile, card
  bottom-right in Disket Mono, thin-lens focus pulls); Art: «все анимации стали однотипные».
  Offer 2–3 distinct directions, recommend one. (persona-belief v1 rejected, «Оттиск» chosen.)
- Approve Тишина first, then the other 6 themes. Never do all themes at once.
- Live HTML prototype instead of video (videos took ~50 min to render and he had to wait).
- Approved changes often ship together. When he says «пушим всё вместе», wait for every part.

## Look
- Light themes: the scene merges completely with the page. A paler rectangle reads as a white
  background («почему все еще белый фон есть?»). Light is never white: «белый цвет там вообще
  не в тему»; ink it in the lamp color, like the amber/umber effects of his light paintings.
- Дюна: no eye-searing saturated orange; soft sepia print.
- Tablet/PC: illustrations as wide as the plate below, never a centered shrunk hero.
- Words placed literally where he says: the tuning-questions conclusion sits at lip height.
- Hairlines must survive 125% display scaling (draw outlines outside the clip path).

## World in glasses (block 1 page 01)
- Gum stays GOLD (pink clashes). No smile at the end. No grey/beige desaturation in the cracks.
- Lens edges come from his hand-drawn mask (`graphics/world-in-glasses/assets/lens-mask.svg`). Never auto-trace lenses.

## Колокол (block 2 page 04)
- Light themes must read as clearly as the 48 s flood frame: «слишком всё сливается» when only faint lamp lines sat on the page. Fix: a quiet light from the mouth and an ink S-curve (lit forms deepen to ink).
