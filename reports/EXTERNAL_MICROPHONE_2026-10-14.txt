External microphone capture — 2026-10-14

Reported by user: Android Chrome/PWA with an OTG wireless microphone receiver.
Recording started but the saved file was silent; Telegram recorded successfully.
The exact cause on that physical phone/receiver is not confirmed remotely.

Previous web implementation requested the browser-selected source without a
device ID, with echoCancellation/noiseSuppression enabled. Diary and architecture
tasks share useObservationRecorder, so the same capture behavior affected both.

Changes:
- Added a browser-only microphone input chooser in Settings; selection persists
  in the existing encrypted settings. The optional field is backwards compatible.
- Refresh the browser-exposed input list on devicechange and after permission.
- Automatic mode prefers available inputs labelled USB/BT/external/headset.
- Explicit choices use deviceId.exact. A failed/disconnected choice is not
  silently replaced with the phone input; readback mismatch is reported.
- Request unprocessed input; do not force sample rate or channel count. A
  compatibility retry preserves the chosen device ID and relaxes processing only.
- Re-enumerate after permission when external devices were initially hidden.
- Cancelled/late streams are stopped; the analyser is not connected to output.
- Warn after 3.5 seconds of no detected samples and in the review of a silent
  recording. Missing/suspended metering does not cause a false silence warning.
- On track ending, stop capture and retain the already-recorded partial draft.
- Warnings do not automatically delete audio or prohibit saving a draft.
- Native recording adapters are unchanged; this fix targets the reported web use.
