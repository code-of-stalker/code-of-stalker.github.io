Panel content scrolling — 2026-10-11

Cause
- The independent scanner-light wrapper introduced an inner flex item with
  flexShrink: 0. Its content could grow beyond the bounded outer panel, so
  the actual scroll viewport grew to the whole text height.
- Reproduced in the served build at 320 x 568: the overview panel was 314px,
  but its inner surface grew to 520px. The scroll viewport was 487px with
  487px of content; scrollTop remained zero. The hint had the same failure:
  a 317px outer panel, 483px inner surface and no internal scroll range.

Correction
- GlassHost's inner surface can now shrink within the outer panel and has
  explicit zero minimum width/height. Intrinsic panels can still grow to
  their content; padding and alignment remain on the inner surface.
- The scanner light remains an independent, non-interactive, contour-clipped
  sibling. No masks, animations, material, quality settings, frame loops,
  renderer resources or Rust/WASM code were changed.

Validation

- Chrome WebGPU: 320 x 568, 375 x 812, 768 x 1024 and 320 x 400, covering all
  three themes. Wheel scrolling and actual emulated touch panning succeeded
  in overflowing overview panels. Tall content that already fits stays still.
- At 320 x 568 the restored overview viewport is 281px with 487px of content;
  wheel offset reached 90px, touch offset 115px. Inner and outer panels are
  both 314px high. Embedded step lists stay within the same panel.
- Hint viewport is 282px with 448px of content, reaches its 166px end offset;
  its fixed heading stays in place and the final line becomes fully visible.
  The bottom fade clears at the end. No ancestors scroll accidentally.
- Scanner light remains visible during reveal, non-interactive, unmasked
  and the same size as the inner and outer panel. It clears at completion.
- Desktop WebKit WebGL2: the same four sizes/themes passed for wheel scrolling,
  bounded overview and embedded step panels, fixed hint headings, final-line
  access, bottom fade and scanner visibility. No browser errors in either
  engine. Touch panning was tested in Chrome, not on a physical iPad.
- Chrome recording-review regression also passed: intrinsic panel layout,
