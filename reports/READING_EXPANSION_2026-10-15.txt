Architecture reading panel — immersive scroll expansion

The opening composition retains the approved illustration and notched StepPanel.
Scrolling consumes the illustration area, extends actual frame edges to the
safe viewport, and continues into normal reading. Reverse scrolling restores
the composition. No text scale, changing line length, or stretched corner cuts.

Implementation:
- ReadingExpansion owns bounded scroll-driven geometry. Counter-insets keep the
  text column fixed; viewport growth and the scroll runway balance each other.
- Minimum runway uses current-frame geometry rather than previous measured
  height, so short text on a tall display cannot clamp scrollTop back to zero.
- Header grows by intrinsic padding, preserving the fitted double notch and
  a persistent accessible close button. Hidden step menus preserve read position.
- ReadingDragSurface drives the same ScrollGate from the hero: mouse wheel on
  web, touch on web/native. No extra scroller or background animation loop.
- Hero fades/moves out and stops its animation when hidden. Inline illustrations
  retain their existing visibility measurement, playback and palettes.
- Existing reading/task boundary gestures, cooldown screen and task layout stay
  intact. No data schema, stored progress, voice or cache-update changes.
