Scroll transition cue placement — 2026-10-11

Problem
The embedded recording screen drew its absolute-bottom cue inside the content
area, above the separate step selector. This overlaid the saved-answer action.

Changes
- Embedded recording sends its cue state to the shared task shell. Only the
  shell draws the cue, below the persistent step selector.
- Both standalone and embedded tasks reserve a 32px bottom inset independent
  of cue visibility. The selector-to-caption gap is 11px at all checked sizes.
- Standalone bottom padding explicitly overrides ProductScreen's inline gutter.
- The step-menu closing overlay now belongs to the task's stacking context;
  the selector's footer is above it, making menu buttons directly clickable.
- Existing progress spring, fades, scroll gating, four question cells and
  popup gesture blockers are retained. Rust graphics and quality settings were
  not changed; the exported WASM hash is unchanged.

Validation
- TypeScript: npx tsc --noEmit passed.
- Expo lint passed.
- Expo web export passed.
- Chrome: actual synthetic-microphone recording/save and saved-answer scroll
  navigation passed. Cue never intersects Add Answer or the step selector.
- Chrome: standalone and embedded layouts at 320x568, 375x812, 768x1024 and
  375x1500 passed; light/neutral saved-answer layouts also passed.
- Chrome and WebKit: all three explanation layouts and all four embedded
  viewport sizes passed. Menus close through ordinary clicks, hide the cue
  while open, and restore it after deliberate scrolling.
- WebKit forced WebGL2: standalone and embedded backward-cue layouts passed;
  no microphone capture was tested in WebKit. Neither browser run substitutes
  for new physical iPad/Android measurements.
- No browser exceptions in either run.
