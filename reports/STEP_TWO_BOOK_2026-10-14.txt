Second step: book and projected hero

Integrated the approved frontal book animation after the last paragraph in the
unlocked second-step reading panel. It is not inserted into the cooldown screen.
The existing panel geometry, header, text and top gallery are unchanged.

The chest light gains 55% halo intensity with a gentle 18% spread just before the
hero fades. The original 14-second opening / projection / closing sequence is
preserved. The scene runs once per mounted step and holds the closed book.

All four palettes are mapped explicitly from ProductTheme: Iskra (dark), Dune
(light), Silence (neutral), Astral (violet). Neutral keeps warm local light,
quieter rays and no particles. Palette changes update CSS tokens over the existing
bridge, preserving elapsed time. The native document is stable across themes.

The scene starts when enough of it is inside ScrollGate's measured viewport.
Offscreen, backgrounded and unfocused views pause without advancing the clock.
It does not capture touches. Reduced motion shows a static open-book composition.
There is no active animation-frame loop after completion or while paused.
