Implementation
- Record Observation opens /diary through Expo Router; it does not start the
  question recorder or mutate practice progress.
- PracticeCooldown owns the locked screen: architecture header/back/close,
  observation-time caption, unlocked selector, timer panel, opening date,
  existing diary glyph and cut-corner action.
- One live gallery and the unavailable step copy sit behind the glass veil.
  Navigation, countdown and diary action remain sharp and interactive.
- Web filters that known background directly, using the shared glass material's
  6px blur and a theme-coloured veil. A sibling backdrop-filter left the canvas
  visually sharp in the Windows WebKit check despite its computed blur value.
- Native uses the installed Expo 57 BlurTargetView/BlurView API. Its economy
  path uses the SDK31+ Android method; the Rust glass remains present.
- Themes, shared Rust gallery/glass rendering, scan reveal, panel contours,
  existing automatic glass/vector quality and test-unlock behavior are retained.
- Expiry uses the existing foreground-aware timer. The opening date is computed
  from the actual deadline in local calendar time, including tomorrow's date.
- The timer area can scroll on shorter screens; the diary action is outside
  that scroller. No background vector spheres were reintroduced.
