Step 2 — two inline animations

After paragraph 2 (reflections / not Him): two copies separate from the central
person over 5.6 seconds. A narrow light band crosses their projected silhouettes;
the copies become faint, retaining their thin projection planes. The inner light
belongs only to the central person.

After paragraph 3 (the only Main Hero): four surrounding contours fade from the
outside inward over 4.8 seconds. The central figure and chest light remain.
The source has one restrained breath of light, then settles.

Both reuse the authored person from the approved book source; there is no new
replacement anatomy. A 0.405px outer contour at the SVG source size keeps the
small silhouette legible. The scenes contain no raster, particles, external
network resources or broad Gaussian blur. Mini-art is 264 x 95.33 CSS pixels
on the 320 x 568 screen, with 12px spacing before the following paragraph.

The book remains after the final paragraph, with its existing 14-second cycle,
chest highlight and closing motion. Panel contours and text are unchanged.

All three scenes use measured visibility in the reading panel, pause when hidden
or unfocused/backgrounded, and stop their RAF loop after completion. Short layout
flashes and rapid scroll-throughs are ignored with a 180ms visibility settle.
Reduced motion shows each mini scene's static final composition. Palettes follow
Iskra, Silence, Dune and Astral through the existing book palette bridge.

build-step-two-inserts.mjs, step-two-inserts-runtime.js, Art adapters and
PracticeIntroScreen.tsx. No dependencies were added.
