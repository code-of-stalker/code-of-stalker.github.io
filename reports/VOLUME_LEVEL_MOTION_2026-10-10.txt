Volume, panel scanner and Architecture motion — 2026-10-10

Implemented
- Recording review: the speaker opens a 0–100% volume slider and a separate
  mute control. The control fits the existing empty space, without moving the
  save/re-record buttons. Colors come from the current theme.
- Web playback owns its media element and a MediaElementSource -> GainNode ->
  destination graph. Gain is adjusted with a 15ms smoothing time constant.
  Playback creates/resumes its context in the play gesture. Native playback
  uses the documented Expo Audio SDK57 volume setter, also on source replacement.
  A prefixed AudioContext is supported; without Web Audio the media setter is
  retained as a compatibility fallback, rather than preventing playback.
- URI changes, re-record, saving and unmount pause media, disconnect gain and
  close the owned context. Backgrounding and leaving the screen pause playback.
- Re-record: review fades out over the existing 250ms modal transition; the
  voice scene returns over 360ms without remounting. The outgoing review retains
  its draft waveform/duration instead of flashing an empty draft.
- Glass scanner explicitly replays on route re-entry. Quality/resolution
  changes do not replay it. Level panels use the same Laboratory soft scanner
  mask, driven by the shared Rust clock, without another GPU glass pass.
- Level expansion/reversal animates content height, opacity and offset over
  460ms. The title row height also interpolates. Closed contents unmount after
  their exit finishes. Transition endpoints are exact, including zero height.
- Guidance keeps the approved parallel spiral. The original 288x156 scene gets
  a 24-unit upper viewport allowance; CSS size/position compensate to retain
  the original artwork scale and location. Once expanded, the body no longer
  clips its upper halo. It remains clipped during the height transition.
- Levels header starts transparent. After actual scroll (>1px) its background
  fades in; returning to the top fades it out. Only threshold changes update
  React state; scroll frames do not cause repeated React renders.
- Level fill uses the original cut-corner SVG contour at its authored opacity.
  Rectangular CSS fill removed. Borders remain 0.25 / selected level 0.75.
- Themes, motion scenario and independent automatic glass/vector quality
  settings are preserved. No dependencies or version pins changed.
